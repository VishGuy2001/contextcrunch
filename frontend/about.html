About ContextCrunch

Why I built this

While working on heavy AI workflows, I started noticing something that wasn’t obvious at first — I wasn’t using these models efficiently.

Across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, the same behaviors kept showing up. Context would quietly fill up mid-session. Conversations would become highly repetitive. Precious tokens were being burned on formatting and phrases I didn’t actually need.

Sometimes I’d lose important context completely. Other times I’d hit limits faster than expected — even on paid plans — and either have to wait hours or pay more just to continue.

The Inefficiency Trap At a small scale, it’s frustrating and slows down your development cycle.
At a larger enterprise scale, it compounds into significant API costs very quickly.

The problem

Most AI platforms offer zero visibility into how your context window is actually being consumed. There's no standard way to track:

  • How much context window you’re consuming in active memory
  • Where semantic redundancies are bloating the thread
  • How efficiently your prompt structures are utilizing token boundaries

Most developers use AI tools daily, but have no real insight into what’s happening behind the scenes of self-attention mechanisms.

What ContextCrunch does

ContextCrunch was built to make the mathematics of context visible, providing real-time transparency and practical optimization:

  • See how efficiently you’re using model memories with visual metrics
  • Identify where token waste is happening on redundant turns
  • Optimize prompt wording to compress size without loss of intent
  • Strikethrough redundancies and rewrite context to keep threads clean

Why this matters

For individuals, it means saving time, avoiding lost context, and learning how to prompt LLMs more effectively.

For engineering teams and enterprises, it means understanding where AI costs are going — and fixing token inefficiencies before scaling codebases into production.

The bigger idea

Most AI platforms focus entirely on output quality.

ContextCrunch focuses on the path to get there — making LLM usage more transparent, mathematically intentional, and cost-efficient.

I built ContextCrunch because I ran into these inefficiencies myself — and there wasn’t a simple tool to measure or solve them. This is both a comprehensive learning playground for anyone working with AI, and a practical utility to optimize your daily workflow.
